Good to know

Good to know, at a glance.

Your existing notes

Older lines may move to the archive. No note is deleted.

How much room it takes

A short briefing, usually under 1,000 tokens: about 0.5% of a 200,000-token window.

Where it works

Windows, macOS and Linux, wherever Claude Code can find Node.js.

One computer

Notes stay on the computer they were made on. They don't sync to your others.

Repositories

Subfolders and worktrees of a git repository use its notebook.

Your own files

It writes only inside Claude Code's own folder, never in your project.

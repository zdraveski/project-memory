A week with project-memory

Monday to Friday, Claude keeps up.

Mon

You tell Claude the bakery's cake prices.

Claude writes them down.

Tue

A new conversation. “Where did we leave off?”

Claude already knows.

Wed

A big task runs so long that the conversation is shortened.

Claude knows what it was doing and carries on.

Thu

A note gets deleted by mistake.

You ask, and it comes back.

Fri

You open a different project.

Claude knows you like short, plain answers.

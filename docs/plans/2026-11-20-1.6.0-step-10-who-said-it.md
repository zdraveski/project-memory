# 1.6.0 Step 10: Who Said It (release 1.5.4) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A note's history names the exchange and the user's words behind each change, `show` does the same for a note by name, and the briefing tells Claude to check the user's own words before saying "you decided".

**Architecture:** One pure function in `recall.mjs` pairs a note's snapshots with journal entries by time and path. `history()` and the `show` branch of `memory.mjs` print its result in one `by:` form. The briefing gains one fixed line in `handoff-log.mjs`.

**Tech Stack:** Node.js 22+ ES modules, `git` (already used for snapshots), `node:assert`. No dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-19-1.6.0-design.md`, sections "Headline 5: Who said it", "Build order" (the paragraph "Order changed on 2026-11-20" and step 10), "Testing". Part of release 1.5.4. Built on the branch of `2026-11-20-1.6.0-step-2-search-filters.md`: it needs `recall.mjs` and `search --asked`.

## Decisions this plan makes (beyond the spec)

- **The snapshot line stays as it is.** It already carries its date (`memory.mjs:707` and `:713`): `  3f2a91c 2026-11-12 14:03  changed`. This step only adds the `by:` line under it.
- **Times compare by the minute.** A journal entry holds its time to the minute (`e.at`, the minute the request was sent). A snapshot's time is the one `git log` already prints (`--date=format:`, the clock of the PC at the commit). Do not change it to `format-local`: an entry's time is the PC's clock at its own moment too.
- **One correction to the spec, made in the spec too.** The spec said "no later than two minutes after this one". An entry's time is the minute its request was sent (`handoff-log.mjs:381`), so it is never later than the snapshot that holds its change. The upper limit is the snapshot's own minute.
- **The rule, exact.** Snapshots of one note, oldest first. For each, the candidates are the entries whose `Changed` holds the note's path, whose time is at or after the minute of the note's previous snapshot (no lower limit for its oldest snapshot) and at or before the minute of this snapshot, and that no older snapshot was given. Candidates sent before this snapshot's minute come first, and the newest of them wins. When there are none, the newest sent in the snapshot's own minute wins. Several candidates left in one minute: the one whose conversation is the note's `originSessionId` wins, otherwise the one later in the day file.
- **Why each part.** A minute is all the journal holds, so an entry in a snapshot's own minute may have been sent before or after it. Before is taken only when nothing older fits; after is why the lower limit includes the previous snapshot's minute. Without "given once", a quick exchange and its snapshot in one minute would also be named for a change by hand that the next snapshot took.
- **One more difference from the spec's sentence.** The spec says "the newest entry at or after the previous snapshot's minute and no later than this one". This plan takes an entry sent before the snapshot's minute over a newer one sent in the snapshot's own minute, for the reason above. List it in the pull request under the decisions beyond the spec.
- **A limit, stated in how-it-works.** A long turn that changes a note, is snapshotted by another session's save in the middle, and changes the note again: the second snapshot says no exchange was found. `shortcut:` an entry names one snapshot only; upgrade if a user reports a wrong "no exchange found" on a note they watched Claude change.
- **"The note's full path"** is `join(dir, file)` and `join(real(dir), file)`. A changed path matches when it equals one of them after the same cleaning `changedFile` does (slashes, case as the journal folds it). No match by the end of a path here: `notes.md` of another project must not count. A path longer than 300 characters was cut when it was saved (`J_PATH`) and finds nothing.
- **`originSessionId`** is read from the note as it is on disk now, once. `front()` (`store.mjs:252`) gains the field `origin` (a string, empty when the note has none). Read `store.mjs:795` and `handoff-log.test.mjs:776-778` for how the harness writes it, and accept it at the top level or indented under `metadata:`, as `type` is.
- **The `by:` text** holds the entry's time, its conversation tag, `Asked` cut to 100 characters through `clip`, and the key in quotes because a key holds spaces. A helper's entry and a private request print as the journal holds them (`Subagent …`, `[private]`).
- **`show <note>` takes a note's file name**, as `history` and `restore` take it: with or without `.md`, resolved by `named()` (`memory.mjs:159`). It is tried only for an argument no entry key matched. With `--all` it prints a block for every store that has the note, without it the first of this project's store and the shared one.
- **The miss text of `show` changes** to name both things it looked for (Task 2). Two tests pin the old text (`memory.test.mjs:1126`, `:3510`): update them.

## Review Focus

## Task 1: `by:` under each snapshot

**Files:**
- Modify: `recall.mjs` (two functions), `store.mjs:252-300` (`front` gains `origin`), `memory.mjs:698-724` (`history`), `memory.mjs:1336` (the call)
- Test: `recall.test.mjs`, `store.test.mjs`, `memory.test.mjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `fold` and `cleanPath(p)` of `recall.mjs` (step 2, Task 1), `inConversation(e, tag)`.
- Produces:
  - `changers(es, paths) -> entry[]`: the entries of `es` whose `changed` holds one of `paths` exactly, in the order given.
  - `whoChanged(snaps, es, origin = '') -> (entry | null)[]`: `snaps` are snapshot times `'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM'`, oldest first; `es` is the result of `changers`, oldest first. One result for each snapshot, by the rule in "Decisions".
  - `byLine(e) -> string` in `memory.mjs`, module level, beside `clip`'s other users: `by: ${e.at} [${e.s}] asked: "${clip(e.asked, 100)}" · key: "${clip(e.key, Infinity)}"`, or for `null`: `by: no exchange found (changed by hand, by a shell command, by a helper or by the plugin's own sorting)`.
  - `history(dirs, file, everywhere = false, max = 30, failed = [])`: `failed` is `main`'s list, passed on to `told()` as `search` passes it.

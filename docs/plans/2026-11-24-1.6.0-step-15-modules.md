# 1.6.0 step 15: privacy, text and transcript modules (plan)

Amended 2026-11-25 after the audit `reports/Audit of 1.6.0 plans 2026-11-25.md` (findings 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 13).

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-24-privacy-and-transcript-modules.md`, part 2.
Decision: `docs/adr/0001-privacy-decided-in-one-module.md`.

**When.** After 1.5.8 has shipped, because this step starts from its `norm`
in `store.mjs`. It comes first in the 1.6.0 order after 1.5.4:
15, 16, 17, 18, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14.

**What it is.** A pure move in three pull requests. After each one, every
existing check passes with no assertion changes. Only imports change in the
tests, plus the file lists in four tests that copy `src` files (PR A item 9).

**How to work.**
- Branch from `master`.
- Run `node tools/check.mjs` after each edit.
- Sign off every commit.
- Keep local mutate batches to about 8 to 12 lines.

## PR A: `src/text.mjs`, then `src/privacy.mjs`

1. **Create `src/text.mjs` first**, with `test/text.test.mjs` (in-process:
   `clip` is stable and keeps its memo, `defuse` and `plain` on fixtures, `fit`
   caps at `SAY` and cuts per line). Move in from `handoff-log.mjs`, as they
   are:
   - `clip` with its memo (`clipped`, `clippedSize`), `cut`, `plain`, `defuse`,
     `fit` and `SAY`.
   - `SAY` goes too, and is exported: `fit` caps at it and the briefing's own
     check (`handoff-log.mjs:1209`) uses it. `BRIEF` and `EXCHANGES` stay.
   - Imports: Node built-ins and `store.mjs` only. Checked at 337077d: `clip`
     needs `cut`, `cut` needs `defuse`, `fit` needs `defuse` and `SAY`, and all
     of them need only `redact` from `store.mjs`. `plain` and `defuse` need
     nothing. No loop.
   - Switch the callers (`handoff-log`, `compact-gate`, `journal`, `memory`,
     and the tests and `tools/fuzz.mjs` where they take `clip`, `plain`,
     `defuse` or `fit` from `handoff-log`, for example
     `test/handoff-log.test.mjs:18`, `:36`, `:1682` and `:1778`) to import from
     `text.mjs`. `compact-gate` and `journal` still take `clip` from
     `handoff-log` today, so this also thins their import.
2. **Write the direct tests first:** `test/privacy.test.mjs`, in-process,
   using the names that will exist.
   - A table over the six sources, each with its file absent, present and
     unreadable (make it a directory).
   - An unreadable file must answer "private" from every exported question.
   - Fullwidth and zero-width tags.
   - The compaction note's looser `<private` check.
   - Pruning: `.private-sessions` keeps the newest 999; `.memory-off` keeps
     what is on disk plus the newest 100, up to 10,000.
3. **Create `src/privacy.mjs`** and move in, as they are:
   - From `handoff-log.mjs`: `PRIVATE`, `PRIVATE_SESSION`, `hushed`, `off`,
     `offLine`, `unreadable`, `FORGOTTEN` and `forgotten`.
   - From `store.mjs`: `QUIET`, `OFF`, `PROJECT_OFF`, `isOff`, `OFF_PERIODS`
     and `wasOff`.
   - **Not** `unsay` and `put`. `put` (`handoff-log.mjs:886`) writes handoff.md
     through `build`, `parse`, `sectionOf`, `render`, `keep` and `PREV`, which
     privacy may not import. They stay in `handoff-log.mjs` as the handoff
     writer, and `journal` and `memory` keep importing `unsay` from there.
   - Imports: Node built-ins and `store.mjs` only.
4. **Apply the one rule.**
   - The module's answers treat "unreadable" as private.
   - Keep a way for the Stop hook to learn that a file was unreadable
     (`handoff-log.mjs:822`, `:827` and `:837` throw so the failure is
     recorded), and keep that behaviour.
   - The retry count is the caller's choice. The default is 3; the git-rule
     hook uses 1.
5. **Write down the deliberate difference.** Move the compaction note's looser
   check into `privacy.mjs` under its own name, with the comment that sits
   with it in `compact-gate.mjs` (the looser check is at `:132` and `:150`).
6. **Switch every caller to `privacy.mjs`:**
   - `compact-gate`, `journal`, `memory`, `memory-tier`, `enforce` (drop its
     copy of the pattern and its `tagged()` reader), and `handoff-log`.
   - The tests and `tools/fuzz.mjs`.
   - No re-exports from `handoff-log` or `store` of anything that moved.
7. **Delete the dead code:** the `try/catch` around `isOff` at
   `compact-gate.mjs:395`.
8. **Update the tools.** Since PR #239 the tools derive their file lists from
   `src/`, so for each of `text.mjs` and `privacy.mjs` add only:
   - a `suites` entry in `tools/mutate.mjs` (`tools/check.mjs` fails without
     it), its import, and `tools/mutants/<name>.mjs`;
   - repointed hand mutants whose file changed (see "What bit before" in
     CONTRIBUTING).

   Decide in PR A whether `privacy.mjs` and `transcript.mjs` join `SHARED` in
   `tools/deploy.mjs:23` (every suite stands on them). Recommended: yes.
9. **Fix the tests that copy `src` files into temp trees.** Add each new module
   that the copied files now import, directly or through another copied file:
   - `test/memory-tier.test.mjs:632` and `:1557`
   - `test/handoff-log.test.mjs:565` and `:3062`

   No assertion changes.
10. **Check the speed.** Run `node tools/bench.mjs` before and after, and put
    both `'PreToolUse git'` numbers in the PR. The limit is 7.

## PR B: `src/transcript.mjs`

1. **Read first.** Read `turns()` (`handoff-log.mjs:276-402`) in full. A grep at
   337077d finds `PRIVATE` at `:341`, `:344` and `:378` and
   `PRIVATE_SESSION` at `:358`. List any privacy branch beyond those in the PR.
2. **Write the direct tests first:** `test/transcript.test.mjs`, in-process.
   - Fixture records, read forward and backward.
   - A line cut at each byte reader's chunk edge.
   - Helper records skipped unless asked for.
   - `turns()` output on the fixtures equals today's output. Capture it from
     master before the move and commit it as the expected value.
3. **Create `src/transcript.mjs`** with two levels, records and turns, and
   move in from `handoff-log.mjs`:
   - `readTail`, `text`, `asked`, `prompt`, `own`, `unwrap`, `queuedText`,
     `toolAnswers`, `refused`, `editedFile`, `turns`, `promptBefore`, `goesOn`,
     `lastTurn` and `transcripts`.
   - The record-reading names `turns()` needs: `FORGETS`, `BARE`, `unquote`,
     `userText`, `nudged`, `NUDGE_TAGS` (with `NUDGE_TAG`, which `compact-gate`
     imports) and `TAIL`.
   - Their private helpers move with them (`stick` and the `F_*` patterns,
     `QUOTES`, `ASKS_SECRET`, `LEAD`, `command`, `image`, `scan` and the
     others a grep shows only these use).
   - `tailOf`, `joined`, `BLOCK`, `SAID` and `SAID_KEEP`: `turns()` and
     `handoff()` (`:794`) both use them, and `journal` and `memory` import
     `BLOCK`. They move here and are exported.
   - There is no "record-type filter" block to move. The first-key filter
     (`:289`, "a record whose first key is a type nothing here reads") is inside
     `turns()` and moves with it.
   - Imports: Node built-ins, `store.mjs`, `privacy.mjs` and `text.mjs` only.
     Checked at 337077d: `turns()` needs `clip` and `plain` (`text.mjs`),
     `PRIVATE` and `PRIVATE_SESSION` (`privacy.mjs`), and `norm`, `local` and
     `redact` (`store.mjs`). Its other names (`keep`, `parse`) are a local and
     `JSON.parse`, not the handoff's. No name needs the handoff writer, so there
     is no loop.
   - What stays in `handoff-log.mjs`: `latest`, `shown`, `handoff`, `put`,
     `unsay`, `build`, `render`, `parse`, `sectionOf`, `stalled`, `main` and the
     rest of the writer and briefing. They import these names from
     `transcript.mjs`.
4. **Fold the byte readers** into the records level, keeping each one's
   limits. That is 7 of the 8 hand-written readers:
   - `handoff-log.mjs:535` (1 MB backwards, up to 256 MB). `readTail`
     (`handoff-log.mjs:50`) moves by name in item 3.
   - `journal.mjs:213`, `:371` and `:525`, and `past` at `:641-701`.
   - `enforce.mjs:134` and `:165`, in PR C.
   - `store.mjs:139` (`startCwd`) does **not** move. `storeOf` (`store.mjs:187`)
     calls it, so moving it would make `store` import `transcript` and
     `transcript` import `store`. It stays as the one byte reader left in
     `store.mjs`.
   - `compact-gate.mjs:120` is a parse loop over what `readTail` returns, not a
     byte reader. It switches to the records level.
5. **Switch the callers** (`handoff-log`, `journal`, `compact-gate`, `memory`,
   the tests, `tools/fuzz.mjs`) to import directly. `store` is not one: it keeps
   `startCwd`.
6. **Check the import loop.**
   - `journal` and `memory` still import `unsay` from `handoff-log`, and
     `compact-gate` imports `handoff` and others, so the loop through
     `handoff-log.mjs`'s run-time imports stays until step 18. That is

   - List in the PR every name they still take from `handoff-log`.
7. **Update the tools.** A `suites` entry for `transcript.mjs` in
   `tools/mutate.mjs`, its import, and `tools/mutants/transcript.mjs`. Repoint

8. **Fix the tests that copy `src` files into temp trees**
   (`test/memory-tier.test.mjs:632` and `:1557`, `test/handoff-log.test.mjs:565`
